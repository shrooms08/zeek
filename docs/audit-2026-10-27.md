# Zeek audit, 2026-10-27

**Update, 2026-10-28:** the high findings C1 and C2 were fixed and deployed
(commits `2c0211e` and `a85e63c`), and the critical findings of audit B were
fixed and deployed by 2026-10-28 (see commit `0be9901`). Still open: F2 (high,
planned for after judging). F1 (high) is covered by funding the operator
float, not yet by a code fix.

Read-only audit of the repo and the live testnet deployment (engine, relay
and keyholders on Railway; site on Cloudflare Pages). Nothing was changed,
committed, deployed or restarted. Live checks were GET requests only.

Severity is about the live game: what a player (or a judge) sees, or what
happens to the vault's funds. "Risk of fix" is the risk the fix itself poses
to the live game.

Every critical and high finding below was re-checked against the code by hand.

- **Code audited:** main at `bc059e5`.
- **Committed during the audit:** `07b7d92` ("fix(verify): require drand
  quicknet beacon key on testnet and mainnet", 18:07) touched
  `crates/zeek-core/src/{beacon,params}.rs` and `crates/zeek-verify`. It is
  not covered beyond what the reviewers read while it was in progress.
- **Disk:** 13 GB free at the end; `target/` is 19 GB.

## Summary

- **No path pays a round twice or loses vault funds.**
- **One funds-liveness bug (high):** a Deep Cache win could not be paid
  without operator float. Covered for now by funding the float.
- **One stuck-payout class (high, rare):** a payout can get stuck and need
  manual recovery.
- **Two high player-facing bugs, both web-only fixes:**
  - a reveal left in a background tab freezes on "Waiting for the draw…";
  - a failed Pocket broadcast says "Nothing was sent" when the dig may have
    landed.
- **Security:** no critical or high findings. One medium: one IP can use up
  the engine's global rate limit for everyone.
- **Mac daemons:** `dev.zeek.engine` and `dev.zeek.keyholder-1..3` are
  disabled in launchd, not loaded, and no zeek process runs (checked with
  `launchctl print-disabled system` and `pgrep`).

## 1. Funds safety

### F1. High: a Deep Cache payout with a winner cannot be paid (details withheld until fixed)

A Deep Cache win could fail to pay until the operator adds funds. No vault
funds are at risk, and the operator float now covers it.

### F2. High (rare): a round stuck forever after the keyholders signed (details withheld until fixed)

In rare failure cases a winner's payout can stay on "paying" and need manual
recovery. Nothing is paid twice and no funds are lost.

### F3. Low: an operator script has no "Railway is live" guard (details withheld until fixed)

An operator script lacks a safety check against running local services
beside the hosted ones.

### F4. Low: Pocket digs can expire later than the round ends (details withheld until fixed)

A delayed dig can land in the next round and be refunded minus a fee.

### Checked and sound (funds)

- **No double payout:**
  - the `Signing` record is written before signing (`coordinator.rs:750-760`);
  - the payout table's primary key blocks a second insert;
  - keyholders persist `signed_rounds`;
  - every payout spends all of its round's dig notes, so the chain itself
    blocks a second payout.
- **FROST nonces:**
  - fresh OS randomness per session, held in memory only;
  - the session is removed before its nonces are used (`signing.rs:72-130`);
  - round 2 is pinned to round 1's sighash.
- **Verify-then-sign:** each keyholder independently:
  - follows the chain and checks drand's BLS signature;
  - recomputes the timing rule and the settlement;
  - opens the receivers;
  - matches outputs exactly;
  - requires exactly one change output;
  - requires an exact ZIP 317 fee;
  - checks the transaction version, branch and expiry;
  - refuses a stale chain view.
- **Settlement:**
  - the conservation check runs on every settlement (`settle.rs:539-549`);
  - rounding dust goes to the Cache;
  - outputs at or below the fee go to the Cache;
  - an untriggerable Cache carries over;
  - the Cache chain is strictly ordered from `initial_cache_zat`.
- **Pocket (`tx.rs`):**
  - exact-balance builder;
  - change to the internal address;
  - pending notes excluded;
  - splits make 2–4 parts of at least 0.02 ZEC, at most 1% fee, no dust.
- **Railway:**
  - one replica per service;
  - keyholders have no public domain;
  - `railway-backup.sh` is read-only (`sqlite3 .backup`).
- **Offline tests, all passing:**

  | Suite | Passed |
  | --- | --- |
  | zeek-core | 70 |
  | engine coordinator | 6 |
  | keyholder `verify_pczt` | 5 |
  | zeek-pocket | 17 |

## 2. Correctness a judge could hit

### C1. High: a reveal in a background tab freezes on "Waiting for the draw…"

- **Where:**
  - `apps/web/src/api/LiveProvider.tsx:105-118`: after 60 s hidden the
    stream pauses. On return only `pending`, `rounds`, `ledger` and
    `campfire` are refreshed, not `["round", n]`.
  - `api/queries.ts:19`: no polling while the stream is open.
  - `main.tsx:15`: no refetch on focus.
- **What goes wrong:**
  1. A judge digs, opens the reveal and switches tabs during the
     15-minute wait.
  2. They come back after the draw. The refreshed pending list no longer has
     the round, so the countdown disappears.
  3. The screen sits on "Waiting for the draw…" with the waiting loop
     playing, until they reload.
  - "Tell me when it lands" never fires either: it needs a hidden tab, and a
    tab hidden for more than 60 s gets no updates.
- **Fix:**
  - add `["round"]` to the refresh list on return;
  - refresh the same list whenever the stream reconnects after the first
    connection.
  - Web-only, a few lines. Risk: very low.

### C2. High: a failed Pocket broadcast says "Nothing was sent" when it may have been

- **Where:**
  - `packages/zeek-pocket/src/worker.ts:208-221`: any broadcast error
    (network reset, the relay's 502 after a timeout) abandons the
    transaction.
  - `apps/web/src/screens/DigPocketScreen.tsx:434` always says "Nothing was
    sent."
  - `recordMyDig` is skipped.
- **What goes wrong:**
  - The node may already hold the transaction, and the dig lands. The player
    was told it didn't, their reveal won't open automatically, and "Try
    again" after it is mined is a second real dig.
  - This contradicts the project rule that nothing false is shown as real.
  - Most likely during an engine redeploy: the engine handles SIGINT but not
    SIGTERM (`bin/zeek-engine.rs:309-339`).
- **Fix now:** for network or relay errors in the broadcast phase, say "We
  couldn't confirm it reached the chain. Check History in a minute before
  trying again."
  - Web-only text change. Risk: very low.
- **Later:** don't `abandon` on ambiguous errors; reconcile from the chain.

### C3. Medium: a dig can name a round that has already closed when the board is stale (details withheld until fixed)

While the board is briefly stale, a dig can go to a round that has closed.
It is refunded by the rules, but the app may count it in that round.

### C4. Medium: "We'll keep trying" is false off the field (details withheld until fixed)

After a short engine outage some screens stay on an error until the player
retries.

### C5. Medium: the round clock shows "0:00 · CLOSED" while the round is still open (details withheld until fixed)

The countdown can show a round as closed shortly before it closes.

### C6. Medium: a late or unreachable drand stalls every reveal with no word (details withheld until fixed)

If the drand beacon is late, reveals wait with no explanation.

### C7. Medium: "Dig as soon as it's ready" never fires (details withheld until fixed)

The option to dig automatically once coins are ready does not work.

### C8. Medium: whether the fauzec.com drip reaches the Pocket is not yet proven (details withheld until fixed)

It is not yet confirmed that the recommended faucet can fund a new Pocket.

### Low (correctness)

- **L1. Low:** a failed payout is retried only after a long backoff. (details withheld until fixed)
- **L2. Low:** old Orchard-pool coins in a restored pre-NU6.3 wallet are not shown. (details withheld until fixed)
- **L3. Low:** raw transport errors can appear in the wallet status line. (details withheld until fixed)
- **L4. Low (suspected):** a duplicate WebSocket can stay open (harmless duplicate events). (details withheld until fixed)
- **L5. Low:** many players behind one network address can fall back to slower updates. (details withheld until fixed)
- **L6. Low:** a gateway error page is reported as "Zeek is very busy". (details withheld until fixed)
- **L7. Low:** Replay's "saved" time shows no date. (details withheld until fixed)
- **L8. Low:** a refund-only round shows "no_winner" while its refund is pending. (details withheld until fixed)

### Checked and sound (correctness)

- **Dig cut-off:** UI, Pocket and engine agree on 4 blocks
  (`readiness.ts:11`, `zeek-pocket/src/tx.rs:47,321`,
  `zeek-core/src/board.rs:118`).
- **Countdowns:** they never go negative.
- **drand:** one beacon formula, shared by engine, keyholders, CLI and
  browser (`zeek-core/src/beacon.rs:145-162`). Hand-checked on round 2002.
  BLS is verified.
- **Engine restart:**
  - SQLite WAL resumes from the stored tip with reorg rollback;
  - landing digs rebuild from the mempool;
  - a new stream gets a board snapshot.
- **Relay restart:** it doesn't affect players. The relay serves only
  Verify's second source; the Pocket syncs through the engine.
- **Replay:**
  - it only starts on offline or busy errors;
  - it is labelled throughout;
  - it leaves by itself;
  - replayed data can't show as live.
- **Late digs:** refunded by the rules; the other-wallet screen labels them.
- **Pocket balance:** split into spendable and confirming correctly, and the
  dig button's state follows it.
- **First-run path:** create, back up, check words, dig works. The load
  screen is silent on deep links and in Replay.

## 3. Security

### S1. Medium: one IP can use up the engine's global rate limit for everyone (details withheld until fixed)

A single client can make the public API refuse requests for every player,
including digs.

### S2. Low: share-card rendering is unbounded (details withheld until fixed)

Heavy use of share-card images can tie up engine memory and CPU.

### S3. Low: proof verification can be forced cheaply (details withheld until fixed)

A few clients can keep proof verification busy, so real winners are told to
wait.

### S4. Low: upstream error text leaks (details withheld until fixed)

Some error responses include internal upstream error text.

### S5. Low: `cargo audit` reports 3 vulnerabilities (details withheld until fixed)

Dependency advisories affect TLS certificate trust. Exploiting them needs a
network attacker and a misissued certificate.

### S6. Low: relay egress can be run up (details withheld until fixed)

Heavy use of the relay could run up its bandwidth bill.

### S7. Low: Campfire impersonation (details withheld until fixed)

Chat users can post under names that look official.

### S8. Low: production CORS allows localhost (details withheld until fixed)

The engine's cross-origin allowlist includes local development origins.
The risk is about zero: there are no cookies or logins.

### S9. Info: rate-limit refusals carry no CORS headers (details withheld until fixed)

A rate-limited browser sees "offline" instead of "too many requests".

### S10. Low (suspected): no header-read or request timeouts on the engine (details withheld until fixed)

Slow clients may be able to hold engine connections open (not verified).

### S11. Info: a legacy proxy path forwards more than the relay needs (details withheld until fixed)

A legacy site proxy path forwards an internal header the relay does not
use.

### Checked and sound (security)

- **Pages Functions:**
  - fixed upstream regex, so no open proxy or SSRF;
  - an anchored list of routes and methods;
  - only allowlisted request headers pass;
  - `CF-Connecting-IP` is required;
  - response content types are allowlisted and get a script-free CSP;
  - bodies are capped at 32 KB and 256 KB;
  - only public 200s are cached.
- **Edge (`edge.rs`):**
  - constant-time secret compare, at least 32 characters;
  - a wrong secret is refused in every mode;
  - proxy headers are stripped;
  - client addresses can't be forged (re-checked live on 2026-10-26).
- **Input bounds:**
  - request bodies are size-limited;
  - block ranges and list lengths are capped;
  - hex-validated ids;
  - u32 overflow refused;
  - a bounded read pool (503 when full).
- **WebSocket:**
  - connections are capped in total and per IP;
  - client frames are capped, and any client data ends the stream;
  - lagging clients skip events.
- **CSP as served** equals `dist/_headers`:
  - `script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'`, `object-src 'none'`,
    `base-uri 'none'`, `frame-ancestors 'none'`;
  - `connect-src` lists only the Railway engine and relay, drand and
    blockchair;
  - COOP, COEP, CORP, nosniff, no-referrer and Permissions-Policy are set.
- **XSS:**
  - Campfire text renders as React text;
  - every `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` is internal SVG or QR output;
  - invisible and bidi characters are hidden;
  - share cards are escaped.
- **No secrets in the deployed bundle:**
  - the live chunk is identical to local `dist`, and all 47 assets grep
    clean;
  - `wrangler.toml` and `.zeek-deploy` hold only public origins and the KV
    id;
  - the proxy secret comes from an env binding.
- **Keyholders:**
  - no Railway domains;
  - they bind only on the private network;
  - `dkg:false`;
  - sign requests are signed by the pinned coordinator key, and replies are
    checked against pinned identities;
  - Railway's WireGuard covers the transport.
  - **To check by hand:** no TCP proxy in Railway Dashboard → Settings →
    Networking for each keyholder.
- **Wallet:**
  - Argon2id (64 MiB, 3 passes) with XChaCha20-Poly1305;
  - the seed is never stored in clear;
  - the clipboard is cleared after 60 s.

## 4. Web performance

**Measured on the live site** (Chromium, cold cache, two runs each), with a
desktop profile and a Fast 3G + 4× CPU profile:

| | Desktop | Fast 3G + 4× CPU |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First paint (load-screen logo) | 1.65–1.86 s | 2.25–2.29 s |
| "Tap to dig in" shown; field rendered | 3.43–3.66 s | 4.83–4.96 s (one cold run 7.16 s) |
| Tap → load screen gone | about 3.4 s (by design) | about 3.5 s |

- Bytes before the tap: 344 KB. After the tap, sounds bring it to 2.37 MB.
- The prompt waits for the engine's board. Its three calls take 0.74–1.25 s:
  0.2–0.4 s at the server, the rest DNS and TLS to Railway.

**The largest assets:**

| Asset | Raw | Served (gzip) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main JS | 536 KB | 178 KB |
| CSS | 66 KB | 24 KB |
| Pocket wasm | 13.0 MB | 2.66 MB (brotli 1.96 MB) |

- Main JS contents: react-dom 25%, framer-motion 28%, the art kit about
  45 KB.
- Every route except the field is lazy.
- The wasm loads only on wallet and dig screens.

**Pocket wasm on the live `/wallet`:**

| Step | Desktop | Fast 3G + 4× CPU |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create form shown | 10.0–14.9 s | 32.8 s |

- **First proof of a session** (desktop):

  | Step | Time |
  | --- | --- |
  | Thread pool | 2.2 s |
  | Proving key | 5.8–6.2 s |
  | Verifying key | 3.8–4.2 s |
  | One-tile proof | 3.7 s |
  | **Total** | **13.9 s** |

- Peak wasm memory is 117 MB.

**Long session:** live field, 10 minutes, garbage collection forced before
each sample.

- **Heap:** 5.3 → 6.6 MB, growing about 0.1 MB per minute after minute 2. At
  that rate an hour would add about 5–6 MB (an extrapolation, not
  measured). This looks like cache churn, not a leak.
- **Stable:** DOM, listeners and one WebSocket held steady.

### P1. Medium: hashed assets are not cached long-term (details withheld until fixed)

Repeat visits re-check every file, which slows loading.

### P2. Medium: the wasm ships a debug `name` section (details withheld until fixed)

The Pocket wasm is larger than it needs to be.

### P3. Medium: the first dig of a session waits for keys (details withheld until fixed)

The first dig in a session is slow while proving keys load.

### P4. Medium: the wallet waits for the thread pool before it can answer anything (details withheld until fixed)

The wallet can't answer until its worker threads start, which delays the
first screen.

### P5. Medium: the enter sound starts late on slow networks (details withheld until fixed)

The load screen's enter sound can start out of time on slow networks.

### P6. Medium: the idle field keeps the CPU busy (details withheld until fixed)

Ambient animations keep the CPU busy while the field is idle, costing
battery on phones.

### P7. Medium: `LiveClient` can leave a duplicate WebSocket open (details withheld until fixed)

A stale WebSocket can keep applying events (harmless today).

### Low (performance)

- **P8.** a QR library is bundled into the main chunk. (details withheld until fixed)
- **P9.** the animation library is bundled in full. (details withheld until fixed)
- **P10.** the app does not open its engine connection early. (details withheld until fixed)
- **P11.** the music file is large and preloaded. (details withheld until fixed)
- **P12.** audio range requests may misbehave on iPhone (untested). (details withheld until fixed)
- **P13.** wallet syncs queue up instead of merging, which can delay a dig. (details withheld until fixed)
- **P14.** a benchmark function ships in the production wasm. (details withheld until fixed)
- **P15.** the variable-width font is large (a design call). (details withheld until fixed)

## 5. Code health

**Clean:**
- no TODO, FIXME, XXX, HACK, `todo!()` or `unimplemented!()` anywhere;
- no `#[allow(dead_code)]`, eslint-disable or ts-ignore;
- no unused files, CSS or web dependencies;

### H3. Medium: test reliability (details withheld until fixed)
