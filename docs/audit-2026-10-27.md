# Zeek audit, 2026-10-27

**Update, 2026-10-28:** the high findings C1 and C2 were fixed and deployed
(commits `2c0211e` and `a85e63c`), and the critical findings of audit B were
fixed and deployed by 2026-10-28 (see commit `0be9901`). Still open: F2 (high,
planned for after judging). F1 (high) is covered by funding the operator
float, not yet by a code fix.

Read-only audit of the repo and the live testnet deployment (engine, relay
and keyholders on Railway; site on Cloudflare Pages). Nothing was changed,
committed, deployed or restarted. Live checks were GET requests only.

Severity is about the live game: what a player (or a judge) sees, or what
happens to the vault's funds. "Risk of fix" is the risk the fix itself poses
to the live game.

Every critical and high finding below was re-checked against the code by hand.

- **Code audited:** main at `bc059e5`.
- **Committed during the audit:** `07b7d92` ("fix(verify): require drand
  quicknet beacon key on testnet and mainnet", 18:07) touched
  `crates/zeek-core/src/{beacon,params}.rs` and `crates/zeek-verify`. It is
  not covered beyond what the reviewers read while it was in progress.
- **Disk:** 13 GB free at the end; `target/` is 19 GB.

## Summary

- **No path pays a round twice or loses vault funds.**
- **One funds-liveness bug (high):** a Deep Cache win cannot be paid today.
  The Cache's money sits in notes the keyholders are designed to refuse.
- **One stuck-payout class (high, rare):** a payout that fails after the
  keyholders signed stays stuck, with no automatic recovery.
- **Two high player-facing bugs, both web-only fixes:**
  - a reveal left in a background tab freezes on "Waiting for the draw…";
  - a failed Pocket broadcast says "Nothing was sent" when the dig may have
    landed.
- **Security:** no critical or high findings. One medium: one IP can use up
  the engine's global rate limit for everyone.
- **Mac daemons:** `dev.zeek.engine` and `dev.zeek.keyholder-1..3` are
  disabled in launchd, not loaded, and no zeek process runs (checked with
  `launchctl print-disabled system` and `pgrep`).

## 1. Funds safety

### F1. High: a Deep Cache payout with a winner cannot be paid

- **Where:**
  - `crates/zeek-engine/src/coordinator.rs:652-670`: float candidates exclude
    every note the game knows.
  - `crates/zeek-keyholder/src/verify.rs:429-435`: keyholders refuse a known
    note that is not a dig of the round.
  - `verify.rs:486-492`: fee ≤ collected fees, which allows at most one
    float note per payout output.
  - `coordinator.rs:533-537`: rounds with no payout are `nothing_to_pay`,
    so their dig notes are never spent.
- **What goes wrong:**
  - The Cache grows mostly from unwon pots, forfeits and shouts. Those stay
    in the vault as dig, forfeit and shout notes that no payout may spend.
  - A Cache payout must therefore come from operator float. Live ledger at
    height 4,548,116 (`/v1/vault/ledger`):

    | Item | zat | ZEC |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Cache | 27,631,000 | 0.276 |
    | Operator float | 3,755,000 | 0.038 |
    | Vault balance | 31,386,000 | 0.314 |

  - The float figure is an upper bound: it includes the 5,000-zat dig fees
    of no-payout rounds, which sit in notes that can't be spent.
  - When the Cache breaks open (1 in 625 per round, about once every 4 days
    at 144 rounds a day) and someone dug the treasure tile, `plan_inputs`
    fails (insufficient), or the keyholders refuse the fee.
  - The round retries every 30 minutes, forever. That winner, their normal
    treasure share and any refunds in the round are not paid.
  - No funds are lost. The keyholders refuse in round 1, so nothing is marked
    signed, and the round pays once float is added.
- **Likelihood:** low per day (Cache trigger × someone on the treasure tile),
  but this is the game's headline jackpot.
- **Smallest safe fix now (no deploy):** add one Ironwood note larger than the
  Cache plus headroom (0.4–0.5 TAZ) to the vault's **internal-scope (change)
  address**, which the vault wallet spends and the game does not "know".
  - **Do not send it to the public vault address.** After genesis that note
    is a forfeit: it is added to the Cache and can never be used as float,
    which makes this worse.
  - The internal address needs the vault's full viewing key, which only the
    operator holds (the public UIVK does not give it).
  - Before sending, confirm the bug on the engine's database: no spendable
    vault note outside `known_note_ids` is as large as the Cache.
  - Risk to the live game: none, if the address is right.
- **Proper fix (after judging; engine, keyholders, verifier):** make the
  Cache's backing spendable. Either:
  - a sweep payout that consolidates no-payout rounds' dig notes into change,
    with keyholders accepting forfeit and shout notes as Cache inputs; or
  - keyholders accept float beyond one note per output against a float they
    compute themselves.
- **Docs:** `docs/mainnet-runbook.md:119-127` funds 0.05 ZEC of float "to the
  vault address". That only works before genesis, and it is far smaller than
  any Cache. Rewrite it with the fix.

### F2. High (rare): a round stuck forever after the keyholders signed

- **Where:**
  - `crates/zeek-engine/src/coordinator.rs:750-804`: the record is
    `Signing`, then there is one broadcast with no retry, and the signed
    transaction is not stored.
  - `coordinator.rs:1269-1272`: a round with any payout record is skipped
    for good.
  - `crates/zeek-keyholder/src/verify.rs:214-217`: a keyholder refuses a
    round it already signed.
  - `signing.rs:121-126`: a keyholder marks the round as signed before it
    releases its shares.
- **What goes wrong:** the winner stays on "paying" and is never paid. The
  notes stay in the vault and nothing is lost. Any of these paths causes it:
  - a broadcast error, or a rejected broadcast (`:799-803`);
  - an engine restart or redeploy between signing and broadcast;
  - the 600 s pass timeout cancelling signing;
  - a database error writing the keyholder events (`:765-772`), which drops
    a signed transaction;
  - round 2 failing after one keyholder has released its share (the record
    is deleted and retried, but too few unmarked keyholders remain);
  - a broadcast that is never mined: the record is marked Paid, and the
    obligation drops out of the ledger at expiry.
- **Checked now:** nothing is stuck. Every recent round owing a payout or
  refund has a payout txid (`/v1/rounds?limit=100`).
- **Mitigation now:**
  - Do not redeploy the engine or keyholders between the log lines "payout
    transaction planned" and "payout broadcast".
  - Write a manual unstick runbook (`ZEEK_HOLD`, clear `signed_rounds` on
    two keyholders and the engine's payout row).
- **Fix (after judging, engine first):**
  - store the raw signed transaction and its expiry before broadcasting;
  - rebroadcast until mined;
  - alert on a `Signing` record older than N minutes;
  - make the event write non-fatal.
- **Later, on the keyholders:**
  - store the expiry with each signed round;
  - allow re-signing once the tip has passed that expiry with the dig notes
    still unspent;
  - cap the expiry (`verify.rs:191` has no upper bound).
- **Risk:** moderate. It needs an engine redeploy.

### F3. Low: `scripts/isolate-services.sh` has no "Railway is live" guard

- **Where:** `scripts/isolate-services.sh` (no `ENGINE_ORIGIN` check, unlike
  `scripts/zeek-up.sh:29-30`).
- **What goes wrong:** run with sudo, it would install and start the Mac
  engine and keyholders beside Railway. Even then, no round can be paid
  twice: every payout must spend all of the round's dig notes, so two
  payouts conflict on chain. The worst case is a stuck record (F2).
  - `scripts/railway-restore.sh` is safe: it requires `--railway-stopped`.
- **Fix:** copy the guard from `zeek-up.sh`. Risk: none (script only).

### F4. Low: Pocket digs expire after 40 blocks, longer than a round

- A dig delayed in the mempool can land in the next round. It is then
  refunded minus 10,000 zat (10% of a minimum dig).
- **Fix:** expire at the window's last block. Needs a wasm rebuild and a web
  deploy.

### Checked and sound (funds)

- **No double payout:**
  - the `Signing` record is written before signing (`coordinator.rs:750-760`);
  - the payout table's primary key blocks a second insert;
  - keyholders persist `signed_rounds`;
  - every payout spends all of its round's dig notes, so the chain itself
    blocks a second payout.
- **FROST nonces:**
  - fresh OS randomness per session, held in memory only;
  - the session is removed before its nonces are used (`signing.rs:72-130`);
  - round 2 is pinned to round 1's sighash.
- **Verify-then-sign:** each keyholder independently:
  - follows the chain and checks drand's BLS signature;
  - recomputes the timing rule and the settlement;
  - opens the receivers;
  - matches outputs exactly;
  - requires exactly one change output;
  - requires an exact ZIP 317 fee;
  - checks the transaction version, branch and expiry;
  - refuses a stale chain view.
- **Settlement:**
  - the conservation check runs on every settlement (`settle.rs:539-549`);
  - rounding dust goes to the Cache;
  - outputs at or below the fee go to the Cache;
  - an untriggerable Cache carries over;
  - the Cache chain is strictly ordered from `initial_cache_zat`.
- **Pocket (`tx.rs`):**
  - exact-balance builder;
  - change to the internal address;
  - pending notes excluded;
  - splits make 2–4 parts of at least 0.02 ZEC, at most 1% fee, no dust.
- **Railway:**
  - one replica per service;
  - keyholders have no public domain;
  - `railway-backup.sh` is read-only (`sqlite3 .backup`).
- **Offline tests, all passing:**

  | Suite | Passed |
  | --- | --- |
  | zeek-core | 70 |
  | engine coordinator | 6 |
  | keyholder `verify_pczt` | 5 |
  | zeek-pocket | 17 |

## 2. Correctness a judge could hit

### C1. High: a reveal in a background tab freezes on "Waiting for the draw…"

- **Where:**
  - `apps/web/src/api/LiveProvider.tsx:105-118`: after 60 s hidden the
    stream pauses. On return only `pending`, `rounds`, `ledger` and
    `campfire` are refreshed, not `["round", n]`.
  - `api/queries.ts:19`: no polling while the stream is open.
  - `main.tsx:15`: no refetch on focus.
- **What goes wrong:**
  1. A judge digs, opens the reveal and switches tabs during the
     15-minute wait.
  2. They come back after the draw. The refreshed pending list no longer has
     the round, so the countdown disappears.
  3. The screen sits on "Waiting for the draw…" with the waiting loop
     playing, until they reload.
  - "Tell me when it lands" never fires either: it needs a hidden tab, and a
    tab hidden for more than 60 s gets no updates.
- **Fix:**
  - add `["round"]` to the refresh list on return;
  - refresh the same list whenever the stream reconnects after the first
    connection.
  - Web-only, a few lines. Risk: very low.

### C2. High: a failed Pocket broadcast says "Nothing was sent" when it may have been

- **Where:**
  - `packages/zeek-pocket/src/worker.ts:208-221`: any broadcast error
    (network reset, the relay's 502 after a timeout) abandons the
    transaction.
  - `apps/web/src/screens/DigPocketScreen.tsx:434` always says "Nothing was
    sent."
  - `recordMyDig` is skipped.
- **What goes wrong:**
  - The node may already hold the transaction, and the dig lands. The player
    was told it didn't, their reveal won't open automatically, and "Try
    again" after it is mined is a second real dig.
  - This contradicts the project rule that nothing false is shown as real.
  - Most likely during an engine redeploy: the engine handles SIGINT but not
    SIGTERM (`bin/zeek-engine.rs:309-339`).
- **Fix now:** for network or relay errors in the broadcast phase, say "We
  couldn't confirm it reached the chain. Check History in a minute before
  trying again."
  - Web-only text change. Risk: very low.
- **Later:** don't `abandon` on ambiguous errors; reconcile from the chain.

### C3. Medium: a dig can name a round that has already closed when the board is stale

- **Where:**
  - `crates/zeek-engine/src/bin/zeek-engine.rs:440-528`: one pass does
    ingest → settle → `pay_next` (proving and signing, about 60 s or more)
    → vault sync → board push, so the board's `blocks_remaining` is stale
    during payouts.
  - `packages/zeek-pocket/src/worker.ts:412-443`: the Pocket trusts
    `board.blocks_remaining`, not its own chain tip.
- **What goes wrong:**
  - The UI shows "≥ 4 blocks left" while the chain has already closed the
    round. The dig is refunded minus fees, which is correct by the rules.
  - But the Pocket records it as a dig in that round. The reveal lists it,
    and on a hit it can show "You found the treasure" with an estimated
    share that never arrives.
- **Fix:**
  - **Web:** clamp blocks left with the Pocket's own `chainTip` and the
    board's `window.end`. Risk: low.
  - **Engine (later):** push ingest and the board before `pay_next`.

### C4. Medium: "We'll keep trying" is false off the field

- **Where:** `apps/web/src/components/ui.tsx:522`. Polling is off for offline
  errors, the comeback probe runs only inside Replay on the field, and a
  stream reopen refreshes nothing.
- **What goes wrong:** after a short engine restart, Reveal and Round detail
  stay on "Can't reach the field" until the player taps Try again.
- **Fix:** the same refresh on reconnect as C1. Web-only.

### C5. Medium: the round clock shows "0:00 · CLOSED" while the round is still open

- **Where:**
  - `apps/web/src/components/TimeLeft.tsx:43-47` stamps "Closed" at zero
    whether or not the round is open.
  - `estimated_close_time` assumes exactly 25 s blocks
    (`processor.rs:283-290`).
- **What goes wrong:** the last block often arrives late. The judge sees
  CLOSED, then "0:25" again.
- **Fix:** stamp only when the round is closed; while open at 0:00 show
  "closing, any block now". Also fix "1 blocks left"
  (`FieldScreen.tsx:219`). Web-only.

### C6. Medium: a late or unreachable drand stalls every reveal with no word

- **Where:** `processor.rs:466-470`. Rounds settle in order; a failed fetch
  retries with backoff up to 300 s and can hold a pass for 40 s.
- **What goes wrong:** the reveal sits at 100% on "Waiting for the draw…".
- **Fix:** after `beacon_time + 120 s` say "The draw is late; your dig is
  safe." Web-only.

### C7. Medium: "Dig as soon as it's ready" never fires

- **Where:** `apps/web/src/screens/DigPocketScreen.tsx:166-185, 501-514`.
  When the coins become spendable, the parent switches to the "Check your
  dig" modal and unmounts the queued component before its effect runs. Its
  countdown also runs about twice as fast.
- **What goes wrong:** this is on the first-dig path: faucet coins need 10
  confirmations, about 4 minutes.
- **Fix:** move the queue state into the parent. Web-only, small.

### C8. Medium: whether the fauzec.com drip reaches the Pocket is not yet proven

- **Known (GET only):**
  - fauzec lists `supported_address_kinds: ["unified", "sapling"]`;
  - its schema knows the Sapling, Orchard and Ironwood pools (NU7-aware);
  - it self-shields coinbase and is on the same chain height.
- **The Pocket's address:** a unified address with only an Orchard receiver.
  Payments to it must go through the Ironwood pool, because the Orchard pool
  has been closed since NU6.3.
- **Unknown:** which pool fauzec's drip output actually uses.
- **If it fails:** fauzec's page shows an error, and the Pocket card waits
  until "Stop waiting". fauzec also has a per-IP cooldown the card doesn't
  mention.
- **Action:** one real claim to a fresh Pocket by the operator (planned). Add
  a line about the per-IP cooldown and "Pay from another wallet" as the
  fallback. Web-only copy.

### Low (correctness)

- **L1.** Payout retry backoff grows to 30 minutes (`coordinator.rs:1290-1310`).
- **L2.** Old-Orchard-pool coins (`orchard_pool_zat`) are never shown. Only a
  restored pre-NU6.3 seed is affected.
- **L3.** Raw transport errors reach the wallet status line
  (`WalletScreen.tsx:680, 698-701`), and the first sync shows "block 0 of 0".
- **L4 (suspected).** A WebSocket can leak if `resume()` runs while
  connecting (`live.ts:73-81, 96-104`). The cost is duplicate events,
  harmless.
- **L5.** At most 8 streams per IP (`api.rs:1001`). A judging room behind one
  NAT falls back to polling and shows "reconnecting".
- **L6.** A Railway 502 HTML page is shown as "Zeek is very busy"
  (`engine.ts:68-70`).
- **L7.** Replay says "saved HH:MM" with no date (`ReplayMode.tsx:178`).
- **L8.** A refund-only round shows "no_winner" while its refund is pending
  (`api.rs:618`).

### Checked and sound (correctness)

- **Dig cut-off:** UI, Pocket and engine agree on 4 blocks
  (`readiness.ts:11`, `zeek-pocket/src/tx.rs:47,321`,
  `zeek-core/src/board.rs:118`).
- **Countdowns:** they never go negative.
- **drand:** one beacon formula, shared by engine, keyholders, CLI and
  browser (`zeek-core/src/beacon.rs:145-162`). Hand-checked on round 2002.
  BLS is verified.
- **Engine restart:**
  - SQLite WAL resumes from the stored tip with reorg rollback;
  - landing digs rebuild from the mempool;
  - a new stream gets a board snapshot.
- **Relay restart:** it doesn't affect players. The relay serves only
  Verify's second source; the Pocket syncs through the engine.
- **Replay:**
  - it only starts on offline or busy errors;
  - it is labelled throughout;
  - it leaves by itself;
  - replayed data can't show as live.
- **Late digs:** refunded by the rules; the other-wallet screen labels them.
- **Pocket balance:** split into spendable and confirming correctly, and the
  dig button's state follows it.
- **First-run path:** create, back up, check words, dig works. The load
  screen is silent on deep links and in Replay.

## 3. Security

### S1. Medium: one IP can use up the engine's global rate limit for everyone

- **Where:**
  - `crates/zeek-engine/src/edge.rs:233-234`: `global.allow(..) &&
    per_client.allow(..)` takes a global token even when the per-client
    check then refuses.
  - The same order is in the relay: `crates/zeek-verify/src/bin/zeek-verify.rs:558`.
    The engine relay's own limiter (`relay.rs:400-401`) has the right order.
- **What goes wrong:**
  - One client sending more than 100 requests per second (6,000/min) to any
    cheap route keeps the global bucket empty.
  - Everyone then gets 429 on the board, rounds, wallet sync and the
    Pocket's broadcast, so digs fail. Streams already open keep working.
  - On the relay, 50 requests per second blocks Verify's second source.
  - Confirmed in code; not load-tested.
- **Fix:**
  - check per-client first, then global, in both places;
  - add a test that a flooding client doesn't block a second client.
  - Risk: two lines, but it needs an engine (and relay) redeploy. See F2
    about redeploying.

### S2. Low: share-card rendering is unbounded

- **Where:** `crates/zeek-engine/src/share.rs:41`: the cache is a plain map
  with no cap.
- **What goes wrong:**
  - `share.rs:264-272` renders with no concurrency limit.
  - Walking `/share/round/{0..2028}.png` costs about 110 MB of memory and
    bursts of CPU that compete with payout proving.
- **Fix:** a render semaphore (503 when busy) and an LRU cap. Engine redeploy.

### S3. Low: proof verification can be forced cheaply

- **Where:** `api.rs:937`: a refused proof is never remembered, and there is
  a per-client limit of 10/min but no global limit.
- **What goes wrong:** a few IPs can keep both verification slots busy, and
  real winners get "busy".
- **Fix:** a global limiter and a short cache of refused proof ids.

### S4. Low: upstream error text leaks

- **Where:** `api.rs:905, 911` (`POST /v1/proofs`) return lightwalletd or
  tonic status text.
- **Fix:** a generic 502, as `RelayState::fail` already does.

### S5. Low: `cargo audit` reports 3 vulnerabilities

- **Advisories:** RUSTSEC-2026-0382 (rustls-webpki 0.103.15),
  RUSTSEC-2026-0381 (webpki-roots 1.0.9) and RUSTSEC-2026-0383
  (webpki-root-certs 1.0.9).
- **What they are:** distrusted emSign intermediates. Reachable in Zeek's TLS
  clients (lightwalletd, drand), but exploiting them needs a man-in-the-middle
  position plus a misissued certificate.
- **Fix:** `cargo update` the three crates, then rebuild and redeploy all
  services after judging.
- **Warnings, not vulnerabilities:**
  - atomic-polyfill unmaintained (already accepted);
  - tokio RUSTSEC-2026-0386 `AsyncFd`, which Zeek doesn't use.
- **pnpm audit:**
  - `--prod` is clean;
  - full: 8 dev-only advisories (undici via jsdom and wrangler, @vitest/mocker
    in `apps/harness`), not shipped.
- `docs/security-review.md` says both audits are clean; that is now out of
  date.

### S6. Low: relay egress can be run up

- `/v1/chain/blocks` with 500 blocks is about 291 KB. At 600 requests/min per
  IP that is about 10 GB per hour per IP.
- **Fix:** a hard usage limit in the Railway dashboard (no deploy).

### S7. Low: Campfire impersonation

- Anyone can post as `[zeek]`, `[admin]` or `[support]`, and seed-phrase bait
  ("send your 24 words") isn't filtered. XSS isn't possible.
- **Fix:** reserve those nicknames and extend `config/campfire-filter.json`.
  Engine redeploy, because the filter is loaded at startup.

### S8. Low: production CORS allows localhost

- **Where:** `scripts/railway-setup.sh:90` allows `http://localhost:4174` and
  `:4175`. Live: the engine echoes `localhost:4174` back, and gives
  `evil.example` no header.
- **Risk:** about zero, since there are no cookies or logins.
- **Fix:** drop them after judging. Changing the variable redeploys the
  engine.

### S9. Info: rate-limit refusals carry no CORS headers

- **Where:** `edge::public` wraps outside `CorsLayer` (`api.rs:301-311`).
- **What goes wrong:** a rate-limited browser sees "offline" instead of "too
  many requests".
- **Fix:** apply CORS outside the edge layer. Engine redeploy.

### S10. Low (suspected): no header-read or request timeouts on the engine

- axum `serve` sets no timer. Railway's edge most likely absorbs
  slow-loris clients; not verified.

### S11. Info: the Pages `/relay` proxy sends the proxy secret to the relay

- The relay ignores it (it has no `--proxy-secret-file`). The app now calls
  the relay directly, so `/relay/*` is legacy.
- **Fix:** send the secret only for the engine, or remove `/relay/*`.
  Web-only.

### Checked and sound (security)

- **Pages Functions:**
  - fixed upstream regex, so no open proxy or SSRF;
  - an anchored list of routes and methods;
  - only allowlisted request headers pass;
  - `CF-Connecting-IP` is required;
  - response content types are allowlisted and get a script-free CSP;
  - bodies are capped at 32 KB and 256 KB;
  - only public 200s are cached.
- **Edge (`edge.rs`):**
  - constant-time secret compare, at least 32 characters;
  - a wrong secret is refused in every mode;
  - proxy headers are stripped;
  - IPv6 is counted per /64.
  - Railway overwrites `X-Real-IP`: re-checked live on 2026-10-26, forged
    values did not get fresh buckets.
- **Input bounds:**
  - body limits of 32 KiB (engine) and 256 KiB (relay);
  - at most 500 blocks per request;
  - rounds and campfire limits clamped to 100;
  - hex-validated ids;
  - u32 overflow refused;
  - a bounded read pool (503 when full).
- **WebSocket:**
  - at most 2,000 connections in total and 8 per IP;
  - client frames capped at 1 KiB, and any client data ends the stream;
  - lagging clients skip events.
- **CSP as served** equals `dist/_headers`:
  - `script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'`, `object-src 'none'`,
    `base-uri 'none'`, `frame-ancestors 'none'`;
  - `connect-src` lists only the Railway engine and relay, drand and
    blockchair;
  - COOP, COEP, CORP, nosniff, no-referrer and Permissions-Policy are set.
- **XSS:**
  - Campfire text renders as React text;
  - every `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` is internal SVG or QR output;
  - invisible and bidi characters are hidden;
  - share cards are escaped.
- **No secrets in the deployed bundle:**
  - the live chunk is identical to local `dist`, and all 47 assets grep
    clean;
  - `wrangler.toml` and `.zeek-deploy` hold only public origins and the KV
    id;
  - the proxy secret comes from an env binding.
- **Keyholders:**
  - no Railway domains;
  - they bind only on the private network;
  - `dkg:false`;
  - sign requests are signed by the pinned coordinator key, and replies are
    checked against pinned identities;
  - Railway's WireGuard covers the transport.
  - **To check by hand:** no TCP proxy in Railway Dashboard → Settings →
    Networking for each keyholder.
- **Wallet:**
  - Argon2id (64 MiB, 3 passes) with XChaCha20-Poly1305;
  - the seed is never stored in clear;
  - the clipboard is cleared after 60 s.

**Measured on the live site** (Chromium, cold cache, two runs each), with a
desktop profile and a Fast 3G + 4× CPU profile:

| | Desktop | Fast 3G + 4× CPU |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First paint (load-screen logo) | 1.65–1.86 s | 2.25–2.29 s |

- Bytes before the tap: 344 KB. After the tap, sounds bring it to 2.37 MB.
- The prompt waits for the engine's board. Its three calls take 0.74–1.25 s:

### P1. Medium: every hashed asset is served `max-age=0, must-revalidate`

- **Where:** `apps/web/vite.config.ts:94-110` (`pagesHeaders()` sets security
  headers only). This covers `/assets/*`, `/sfx/*` and the wasm.
- **What goes wrong:**
  - every repeat visit re-checks each file;
  - the JS 304 sits on the critical path (about 0.6 s on Fast 3G);
  - the wasm and music revalidate on every visit.
- **Fix:** `/assets/*` gets `public, max-age=31536000, immutable`; `/sfx/*`
  gets `max-age=86400` (names not hashed). Web-only. Risk: low.

### P2. Medium: the wasm ships a 6.2 MB debug `name` section

- **Where:** `scripts/build-wasm.sh` (no strip, no wasm-opt). The section is
  47% of the raw bytes.
- **What goes wrong:**
  - 150–300 KB more to download, about 1–2 s on Fast 3G;
  - double the module bytes held in the worker and its 8 threads.
- **Fix:** `-C strip=symbols` or `wasm-tools strip`. It changes the Pocket
  wasm, so run the full e2e suite. Risk: low to medium.
- `docs/browser-wallet.md` still says 9.1 MB / 2.1 MB.

### P3. Medium: the first dig of a session waits about 10 s for keys

- **Where:** `crates/zeek-pocket/src/tx.rs:55-64` (a `OnceLock` on the first
  build).
- **Fix:** warm the keys after unlock, or when the Dig screen opens.
  `docs/browser-wallet.md:115` leaves out the verifying key's 4 s.
- **Risk:** CPU contention with sync.

### P4. Medium: the wallet waits for the thread pool before it can answer anything

- **Where:** `packages/zeek-pocket/src/worker.ts:79-91` awaits
  `initThreadPool` (2.2 s on desktop, 3.8 s throttled) before status, create
  or unlock can answer.
- **Fix:** await the pool only before sync or proving. Risk: low to medium.

### P5. Medium: the enter sound starts late on slow networks

- **Where:** `src/boot.ts`. `enter.mp3` is fetched only at the tap, alongside
  13 other files.
- **What goes wrong:** it starts 0.34 s after the tap on desktop and 1.46 s
  on Fast 3G, so the exit sequence plays out of time with it.
- **Fix:** preload `enter.mp3` when the prompt shows (loading needs no
  gesture). Web-only. Risk: low.

### P6. Medium: the idle field keeps the CPU busy

- **Where:** the infinite CSS animations on SVG children (`styles/index.css`:
  zk-drift, zk-twinkle, zk-flicker, zk-sway…).
- **What goes wrong:** about 5 s of main-thread time per minute (8% of a
  core on an M-series Mac), from style, layout and paint every frame. On a
  phone that is likely a third of a core (an extrapolation): battery and
  heat.
- **Fix:** pause ambient loops after a few idle minutes, or move them to
  composited layers. Visual only.

### P7. Medium: `LiveClient` can leave a duplicate WebSocket open

- **Where:** `apps/web/src/api/live.ts` (`resume()` closes the old socket
  with its handlers still attached).
- **What goes wrong:** the late `onclose` reconnects, and an orphan socket
  keeps applying events. Doubled events are harmless today.
- **Fix:** ignore events from a socket that is no longer current. Web-only.
  Risk: low. This is the same as correctness L4.

### Low (performance)

- **P8.** `qrcode` (about 8 KB gzipped) is in the main chunk through the
  `@zeek/pocket` barrel. Fix: add `"sideEffects": false` there.
- **P9.** framer-motion's full `motion` is 28% of the main chunk.
  `LazyMotion` + `m` would save about 15–25 KB gzipped. Medium risk.
- **P10.** No `preconnect` to the engine origin. It would likely save
  0.3–0.5 s of the 0.74–1.25 s engine wait.
- **P11.** `music.mp3` is 1.9 MB at 124 kbps with `preload="auto"`: 11.8 s on
  Fast 3G, competing with the wasm. Fix: re-encode at 96 kbps, or mono at
  64 kbps, and preload metadata only. `miss.mp3` is the only stereo effect.
- **P12.** Cloudflare Pages answers range requests with 200, not 206. Test
  music and loops on a real iPhone.
- **P13.** The Pocket worker runs one call at a time and doesn't merge
  `sync()` calls from 4 timers (`worker.ts:714`). On a slow relay the queue
  grows, and a dig waits behind stale syncs.
- **P14.** `bench_prove` ships in the production wasm.
- **P15.** The 91 KB Archivo width font. This is a design call.

### H1. Medium: the web fee model assumes one input

- **Where:**
  - `apps/web/src/screens/WalletScreen.tsx:542` (`fee = 10_000n`, "one
    input") and Max at `:606`;
  - `lib/digPlan.ts:27`, `lib/pro.ts:16-25`, `DigPocketScreen.tsx:27`.
- **The Pocket's real fee:** `5000 · max(2, spends, outputs)`
  (`crates/zeek-pocket/src/tx.rs:126-160`). The Pocket splits a deposit into
  2–4 coins.
- **What players see (confirmed):**
  - After a 0.1 ZEC deposit splits into 4 coins, **Withdraw Max and Pro MAX
    are refused** with "not enough spendable funds" (the fee is 20,000, not
    10,000).
  - A one-tile dig from 3 coins shows a 10,000 fee and pays 15,000.
  - It is safe for funds: the Pocket refuses before building.
- **Fix:** derive the fee from `spendable_notes`, as `notesCoverPlan`
  (`digPlan.ts:95`) already does, or add a wasm quote export. Web-only.
  Risk: low to medium.

### H2. Medium: the reveal's "Your share" is gross

- **Where:** `apps/web/src/machines/reveal.ts:102-110`.
- **What goes wrong:**
  - The share leaves out the 5,000-zat output fee and the per-dig rounding

  - `ResultCard.tsx:73` "including X from the Deep Cache" shows all winners'

  settlement. Web-only.

### H3. Medium: test reliability

- **`Field.test.tsx` doesn't clean up.** Vitest `globals` is off, so Testing
  Library's auto-cleanup never runs, and `Field.tsx:173-180`'s

  1. Fix: `afterEach(cleanup)` or a setup file.
- **Smoke and smoke-webkit use Playwright's default 30 s test timeout**

  written inside them, which explains many "slow network" failures. Fix:

- **Checks right after a reload use the default 5 s wait** while the board

  - `route.fetch` has no timeout (5 specs);
